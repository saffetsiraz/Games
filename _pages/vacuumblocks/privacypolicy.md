# Privacy Policy for Vacuum Blocks Puzzle Game

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Vacuum Blocks Puzzle Game (the "Game"), published by Lucuxis ("we", "us", "our"), and how you can control it. Contact: saffet57siraz@gmail.com.

## 1. Summary
- The Game has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or contacts.
- Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, lives, settings) is stored on your device.
- The Game shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Our advertising, analytics and purchase partners collect some device and usage information as described below.
- Where the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy Options.

## 2. Information stored on your device
Game progress, settings and your privacy choices are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may keep a copy of this data in your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the Game; this backup is managed by Google under its own policy. Uninstalling the Game deletes the data on the device.

## 3. Advertising
The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads. Unity Ads and AppLovin may also serve ads through AdMob mediation. These partners collect, on their own servers, information such as:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers (for example the app set ID),
- your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
- device information (model, operating system, language) and diagnostics,
- your interactions with ads (views, taps, rewarded ads watched).

They use it to show ads, to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud and, if you allow it, to personalise ads. Each partner processes this data under its own privacy policy:
- Google: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- Unity: [unity.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- AppLovin: [applovin.com/privacy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/)

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

## 4. Consent and your choices
- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalised. If you do not agree, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. If you do not allow information to be stored on your device, the Game also does not use analytics, crash reporting or remote configuration (sections 5 and 6).
- **United States:** residents of states with privacy laws can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. The Game passes your choice on to our ad partners.
- You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy Options in the Game. The option appears where the law requires it.

## 5. Analytics and crash reports
The Game uses Google Firebase:
- **Firebase Analytics** records how the Game is used (for example level started or completed, booster used, store opened, ad watched, purchase made) together with an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how the Game was installed.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** collects crash reports: the error, the state of the Game at that moment, device information and an installation identifier.

We use this information to fix problems and to improve the Game and its balance. Analytics and crash reporting start only after the consent step described in section 4. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months; crash reports are kept for 90 days. Firebase privacy information: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 6. Remote configuration
The Game uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings (such as rewards, prices, offers and ad frequency) without an app update. The request sends the Firebase installation ID and basic device information (country, language, time zone, operating system, app version). It follows the same consent rule as analytics.

## 7. In-app purchases
Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing process information about your purchases (product, time, transaction identifiers) and device identifiers to deliver and verify them, under their own privacy policies ([Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Unity](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)).

## 8. Notifications
If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full or a daily reward is ready). These notifications are created on the device itself; no notification token or message content is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your Android settings.

## 9. Children
The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us at saffet57siraz@gmail.com.

## 10. Data transfers and security
Our partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with the safeguards described in their policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

## 11. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the Game has no accounts, we cannot link data to you by name. You can withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy Options, reset your advertising ID, or uninstall the Game. For any other request, write to saffet57siraz@gmail.com; we will answer and pass deletion requests on to our partners where possible.

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy, for example when we add or remove a partner. The date at the top shows the latest version.
